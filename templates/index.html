ABOUT THE PROJECT

A lens on language.

TruthLens is an educational news-text classification project built to make machine-learning outputs approachable and transparent.

01THE METHOD

From words to a prediction

The app combines TF-IDF text features with a Logistic Regression classifier trained on labeled examples.

01InputA headline or article is submitted for analysis.
02FeaturesThe fitted vectorizer maps words and phrases into numeric features.
03OutputThe classifier estimates a label and class probability.
04HistoryPredictions are saved in a local SQLite database.
02IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

A prediction is not a fact-check

The model may learn patterns that do not correspond to factual accuracy. Treat its output as a starting point for independent research.

!Dataset labelsTraining labels can be incomplete, biased, or incorrect.
!Changing eventsNew topics, sources, or styles may be misclassified.
!Probability meaningScores are model estimates, not the chance a claim is true.
!PrivacySubmitted text is stored in local prediction history.
